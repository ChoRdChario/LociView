## Accepted boundary

include §6.1 Review-copy inspection and §6.2 integrity sharing inside one
outputReady. General §6 payload sharing is not approved. Do not extend these
lifetimes to a whole Session, source capture, write/readback or recovery.

approved2026-11-09: one normal selected-generation public read may share readonly
ports until its closing audit; it adopts only afterward, including readonly and
restore-pending reads. A fresh successful Backup may display its actual new row
after both outer fences while retaining old rows/bases until full discovery.
Failure/retry/cold discovery and fresh download checks stay strict; old history
damage may be detected at the next full inquiry. No staging consumer is added.
[06e §9](../docs/specs/06e-selected-generation-output-read-proposal.md) records
ordinary read-only presentation, UI completion and cooperative-check cleanup;
it grants no new inspection lifetime. Preserve full Host preparation, owned-byte
SHA/CRC and write/recovery fences. Todo owns measurements and final qualification.
[06f](../docs/specs/06f-opfs-parent-lookup-proposal.md) is approved2026-11-10 for
one native parent-location hint in OpfsFS readonly methods. Each file handle/File
and integrity/fence remains fresh; writes/list/exists/exclusive cleanup keep the
original lookup. Resolve is locator matching, not existence/ABA proof. Follow
its ownership/error rules; bounded Windows qualification completed2026-11-11,
with exact measurements/device exclusions in todo. The06e §9.1/§10 evaluations
were not adopted; do not reconnect their code or inspection exceptions.
[09 §10](../docs/specs/09-desktop-offline-candidate-proposal.md) permits exact
same-build/SRI boot-resource repair; it does not permit arbitrary network fallback,
foreign cache/Project writes or forced activation. Follow their recorded contracts
and todo rather than renewing an approval from historical prose.

[01i §7.0](../docs/specs/01i-high-resolution-pc-workflow-proposal.md) authorizes
transient thumbnails and coarse-pointer image/3D residency, including canonical
media-add/retry preparation. Original bytes, native-quality explicit originals,
offline use and save/recovery fences stay unchanged. Owned idle must drain native
cleanup successfully; a finished task alone is not proof of released resources.
Review copy/Scene-add also retire their owned3D and await idle before handoff;
initial readonly sniff/ZIP classification retains cancel ownership through pending
native reads. This does not change storage/publication/recovery checks or introduce
a Safari RAM guarantee or persistent derivative cache. UI/display retirement now
propagates through Project changes, restoration and delayed renderer replacement;
a failed retirement is retained for that selector lifetime until reload.

failures or unresolved concerns, not for staging or result-text updates.

Keep private originals/audits and all.artifacts local. AGENTS.md authorizes suitable
