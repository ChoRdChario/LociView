# Sponsorship and supporter acknowledgement proposal

> Status: `PRODUCT-OWNER APPROVED DIRECTION / PROPOSED OPERATIONAL POLICY`
>
> No sponsor has been accepted and no placement, consultation, payment or
> support obligation is created by this proposal.

## 1. Purpose

LociView creates sponsor value by remaining a trustworthy, freedom-respecting,
offline-capable project. A sponsor is acknowledged for helping that public work
continue. Sponsorship does not buy back functions withheld from other users and
does not convert the sponsor into an owner, author, approver or endorser of the
software.

The preferred acknowledgement phrase is `Supported by`, not `Sponsored by`, to
reduce confusion about ownership, endorsement and control.

## 2. Sponsor benefits

An accepted sponsor may receive:

- time-bounded acknowledgement in the official About/Credits view;
- acknowledgement in the repository README, official project site, relevant
  release notes and a historical funding record;
- a small static `Supported by ...` line on the Home screen only after an exact
  placement receives Product Owner and release approval; and
- individual consultation with the project owner under the boundary below.

These benefits are additive services and acknowledgements. They do not change
the software license or the functionality available to any other user.

## 3. Individual consultation boundary

Sponsor consultation may cover LociView use, offline deployment, LociMyu
migration, data preparation, product workflow, public roadmap context and
technically feasible integration approaches.

Unless a separate written support or services agreement says otherwise:

- consultation is scheduled subject to published capacity and is best effort;
- it provides no response-time, implementation, compatibility, performance,
  legal, security or release guarantee;
- a suggestion enters the same specification, acceptance, security, review and
  gate process as any other proposal;
- the sponsor receives no exclusive feature, private release, roadmap priority,
  gate credit, release veto or right to suppress an observed defect;
- the consultation does not require access to user projects, operational data,
  credentials, device identifiers or analytics; and
- confidential or personal material is not accepted without a separate approved
  confidentiality, retention, deletion and secure-transfer agreement.

A separately paid implementation, migration, training or support engagement may
define deliverables and an SLA for the additional labor. Any project-owned
change accepted into official LociView remains available without sponsor-
exclusive rights under its applicable approved project license and passes the
normal gates.

## 4. Display and interaction rules

Sponsor acknowledgement is static, bundled into the reviewed release and fully
available offline. It does not use:

- an advertising network, rotating remote content or a remote logo;
- tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, affiliate identifiers or campaign/UTM query
  parameters;
- behavioral targeting, install counting or application-initiated sponsor-side
  telemetry;
- a blocking splash, modal, notification, watermark, nag, countdown or repeated
  prompt; or
- a runtime dependency on a sponsor service.

Acknowledgement content is limited to the neutral phrase `Supported by`, the
approved sponsor display name, and an optional licensed logo or direct link. It
contains no slogan, product claim, offer, discount, promotional copy or call to
action. Text is the default. A logo or external link requires proof of
permission for the exact asset/name and a separate release review.

A permitted link is a direct HTTPS destination, opens only after an explicit
user action and uses browser protections that do not send unnecessary referrer
or opener state. Before that action, adjacent plain-language text must say that
the user will leave LociView and that the destination will receive ordinary
network metadata such as the IP address, user-agent and request time. The link
must carry no application/user identifier, affiliate token or campaign
parameter.

Acknowledgement is not inserted into the viewer canvas, editing controls,
import/export/recovery/error flows, project packages, CSV/XLSX, captions,
models, images, screenshots, videos, portable metadata or user filenames.

## 5. Independence and non-endorsement

A sponsor cannot direct or veto:

- architecture, roadmap acceptance, support wording or compatibility policy;
- privacy, security, data-preservation or migration safeguards;
- fixture/source/license adoption or independent review;
- test, measurement, evidence, gate or release results;
- vulnerability disclosure or the recording of known defects; or
- adoption of an account, cloud, advertisement, exclusive format or vendor
  dependency.

A sponsor may fund a disclosed workstream or propose a use case. Funding does
not relax its specification, acceptance, evidence or review requirements.
Acknowledgement must not imply that LociView endorses the sponsor, or that the
sponsor endorses every LociView claim. Third-party author and license credits
remain separate and cannot be replaced or reduced by sponsor placement.

## 6. Privacy and data boundary

No sponsor receives application-collected or automatically disclosed user data
through sponsorship. LociView does not automatically send project contents,
filenames, usage history, install counts, device information, contact details or
other telemetry to a sponsor. The official application remains account-
independent and does not add a sponsor-facing data path. If a user voluntarily
follows an approved external sponsor link, the destination receives the ordinary
network metadata disclosed above; that navigation is not represented as
anonymous and is governed by the destination's privacy terms after navigation.

Consultation records are handled as service communications, not application
telemetry. Their collection, confidentiality, retention and deletion terms must
be stated before a consultation that involves non-public information.

## 7. Transparency and conflicts

Before sponsorship or support with a public name/brand acknowledgement is
accepted, the public funding record must identify, at minimum:

- the sponsor's approved display name;
- the support period;
- a published tier or amount band, and whether support is cash or in kind;
- any disclosed purpose restriction; and
- material conflicts of interest.

Names within the same tier are treated consistently rather than reordered by
private negotiation. Historical support is archived instead of silently erased.
Individual supporters are named only with opt-in. Anonymous donations receive no
brand placement and are recorded either in aggregate or as explicitly anonymous
funding, never under an invented sponsor identity. Public retention of an
individual's historical identity is subject to consent, correction, withdrawal
and privacy terms approved before acceptance. A public entry may be corrected,
annotated or anonymized when those terms require it; legally required financial
records may be retained separately and non-publicly.

LociView may reject or end support whose conditions conflict with law, privacy,
security, data preservation, non-discrimination, review independence or this
policy. Borderline acceptance, removal and refund rules require a later public
operational contract; the Product Owner does not invent them during an active
release.

## 8. Release boundary

Adding or changing a sponsor acknowledgement is release metadata work, not an
out-of-band advertisement edit. Before publication it requires:

- exact approved name, wording, dates, asset and destination;
- trademark and asset-permission review;
- proof that no remote dependency, tracking field or secret was added;
- offline/CSP/build and accessibility/layout verification on the relevant
  release targets;
- verification that project/export bytes and core recovery flows are unchanged;
- independent review of the influence and privacy boundary; and
- the same exact-commit approval, deploy and rollback controls as the release.

Sponsor support itself is not G0/G0-S evidence, gate credit, a support guarantee
or permission to bypass a release stop condition.

## 9. Operational decisions still required

Before accepting any named or anonymous funds, the Product Owner must separately
approve the receiving/payment mechanism and contracting/accounting identity;
privacy and recordkeeping; prohibited-source/conflict screening; termination and
refund handling; and the exact public amount, amount-band or aggregate disclosure
rule. Public anonymity is a display choice, not an exemption from payment-
processor, tax/accounting, sanctions or other lawfully required non-public
records. This proposal does not authorize accepting anonymous donations until
those common rules are adopted.

Before accepting a benefit-bearing sponsor, the Product Owner must also approve:

- sponsor tiers, consultation allocation, scheduling and fair-use limits;
- About-only versus Home acknowledgement for each tier;
- text, logo and direct-link eligibility;
- sponsor acceptance, conflict, termination, refund and appeal rules; and
- any separate confidentiality, professional-services or support agreement.

These operational decisions do not block the proposed open-software license or
the current G0/G0-S engineering work.
